Matatag Productions is the official media and documentation team of Corcuera National High School, dedicated to delivering high-quality content that showcases the vibrancy and achievements of our CorNaHiS community. As a student-led organization, we are committed to excellence in photography, videography, graphic design, and digital media production, ensuring that every milestone and event is documented and shared with the wider school community and our stakeholders.

Matatag Productions serves as a dedicated training ground for students who are interested in digital media and visual storytelling. We believe that a student's creative vision and resourcefulness matter far more than having access to expensive equipment. Our team focuses on maximizing available resources, often utilizing mobile phones to provide comprehensive event coverage for the school.

Students with no prior experience are completely welcome to apply. As long as you have the willingness to learn, we will train you from the ground up. By joining the team, you will gain hands-on experience in using professional editing and layout software. Beyond documenting school activities, our members also participate in community initiatives, such as local tourism projects and advocacy photojournalism, allowing them to apply their skills in practical settings. Whether you wish to focus on photography, videography, graphic design, or social media management, there is a place for you to develop your skills here.
