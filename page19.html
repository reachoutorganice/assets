Schools Press Conference (Journalism Contest)

A campus journalism event where students receive training, participate in competitions, and showcase their skills in various fields, including writing, broadcasting, and photojournalism. The School-Based Press Conference is usually conducted within the first few months of the school year, while the National Schools Press Conference is typically held toward the end of the school year. The activity is intended for campus journalists, student participants, coaches, and teacher-advisers.

Coverage of School Events and Extracurricular Activities

Involves gathering, documenting, and reporting on official school activities, including academic, athletic, cultural, and social events, to keep the school community informed and updated. This activity is conducted throughout the school year, depending on the schedule of school events and extracurricular activities, and involves campus journalists, teachers, and students.

Award for Student Organization Achievement

The Deep Limits received the Award for Student Organization Achievement during the Corcuera National High School Awards for School Years 2024–2025 and 2025–2026. This recognition was given in appreciation of the organization's contributions to the school community, particularly through its school-related initiatives, activities, and participation and achievements in various journalistic competitions.
