Sci-Math Week

Sci-Math Week is a school-wide celebration of Science and Mathematics that allows students to showcase their talents, expand their knowledge, and develop their skills in both subjects. It challenges students' creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving abilities while promoting innovation and environmental cleanliness through fun and engaging activities. The event was held from September 21 to October 2, 2026, and involved the entire school community.

HIRAYA (Leadership Training Program)

HIRAYA is a school-based leadership training program that brings together club officers and the Supreme Secondary Learner Government (SSLG) to develop their leadership skills and sense of responsibility. The program encourages student leaders to become more committed to their duties, strengthen their teamwork and communication skills, and become responsible role models within the school community. It was held on June 14, 2026, where participants received a Participation Award for their involvement.
