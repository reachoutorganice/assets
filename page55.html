Mission

To serve as the voice and representative of the student body by promoting servant leadership, integrity, inclusivity, and active student participation through purposeful programs and initiatives that contribute to a better school community.

Vision

A united, empowered, and responsible student community where every learner is heard, valued, and inspired to lead, serve, and create meaningful change.

Main Objectives / Goals

- Promote Student Leadership – Develop responsible, competent, and service-oriented student leaders.
- Represent Learners' Voices – Uphold students' rights, interests, concerns, and welfare.
- Encourage Active Participation – Engage learners in school activities, decision-making, and community initiatives.
- Foster a Positive Environment – Promote discipline, respect, unity, and environmental responsibility.
- Strengthen Student Organizations – Support collaboration and strengthen student-led initiatives.
- Uphold Integrity and Accountability – Ensure transparency, commitment, and responsibility in every undertaking.
