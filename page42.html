Be a Member of Sports Club!
Who can join the organization?

All students who are interested in sports, physical activities, and developing their athletic skills. 

Membership Requirements

Must be a currently enrolled student of the school; must submit a completed membership form; must have a signed parental consent form; must maintain good academic standing.

How can students join? 

Students may sign up during our recruitment drive or visit us at the designated booth during school events. They may also approach any club officer or our adviser to submit their membership form. 

Recruitment / Application Period

Every start of the school year — usually from September to October. Late applications may be accepted upon consultation with the club officers.
