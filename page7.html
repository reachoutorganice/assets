Get to know us!

OrgANice is a centralized online platform designed to showcase the different student organizations and clubs of Corcuera National High School. It serves as a space where students, teachers, and other members of the school community can discover the identities, activities, and contributions of each organization.

Mission

To provide an accessible and organized platform that connects students with school organizations by presenting their information, activities, achievements, and membership opportunities.

Vision

To become a reliable and engaging digital directory that strengthens student involvement, promotes organizational awareness, and fosters a more connected and participative school community.

Why OrgANice?

Finding an organization that matches one's interests should be easy and accessible. However, students may not always know what organizations are available, what they do, or how to become members.

ORGAnice addresses this by bringing organizational information into one platform, allowing students to discover different communities, explore their opportunities, and make informed choices about getting involved.
