Be a Member of MP!
Who can join the organization?

Bona fide students of Corcuera National High School, particularly aspiring multimedia practitioners and creatives. 

Membership Requirements

Interested students must have a passion for media and digital storytelling, and a willingness to undergo hands-on technical skills workshops covering photography, videography, and graphic design. Prior experience is not required; beginners are completely welcome as long as they are willing to learn. 

How can students join? 

Filling out the official Membership Application Form is the first step. Students may approach any current MP member, the officers, or the Production Manager to be guided through the process. After submission, all applicants will undergo a short, friendly screening and interview process to assign them on their tasks.  

Recruitment / Application Period

Typically held at the Beginning of the School Year
