Welcoming Outreach

Welcoming Outreach is an activity of the Public Campus Ministry that aims to make students feel accepted, valued, and comfortable within the ministry. It focuses on building friendships, welcoming newcomers, and creating a warm and inclusive environment where students can connect with one another and experience God's love.

Room-to-Room Prayer

Room-to-Room Prayer is an outreach activity conducted before the summative examinations, where Public Campus Ministry members visit different classrooms to offer prayers for students. The activity aims to encourage students, ease their worries, and remind them to seek God's guidance, wisdom, and strength as they prepare for their examinations.

Teacher's Day Appreciation

Teacher's Day Appreciation is an outreach activity that expresses gratitude and appreciation to teachers for their dedication, patience, and hard work in educating and guiding students. Through heartfelt musical performances and the simple gesture of giving chocolates, Public Campus Ministry members show their love and recognition for teachers and their important role in shaping students' lives.
