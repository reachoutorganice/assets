Buwan ng Wika Celebration

Is conducted annually every August through a school-wide program featuring various contests and cultural presentations that promote appreciation of the Filipino language, literature, and culture. The activity is open to all students of the school.

Pagbasa and Storytelling Sessions

Are conducted to encourage students to develop their reading fluency and appreciation for Filipino literature through engaging reading activities and storytelling. These sessions are intended for interested students and may be held monthly or weekly.

Club Meetings and Orientation

Are conducted to facilitate organizational planning, officer discussions, and member training. These meetings are usually held monthly and are attended by the club's members and officers.
