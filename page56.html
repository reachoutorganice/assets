Club Elections

Club Elections is an electoral process that allows learners to elect their respective club officers. It promotes democratic participation, student representation, and leadership development by giving students the opportunity to choose leaders who will represent and serve their organizations. The activity was conducted on June 11, 2026, and involved the entire school community.

HIRAYA 2026: Leadership Training

HIRAYA is a leadership development program designed to equip student leaders with essential leadership skills and strengthen their sense of responsibility. It aims to instill the values of integrity, accountability, teamwork, and purposeful service among student leaders. The program was held on June 14, 2026, with student leaders of Corcuera National High School as participants.

Acquaintance Party

The Acquaintance Party is a school-wide social gathering that promotes camaraderie, friendship, inclusivity, and unity among learners. It provides students with opportunities to meet new people, strengthen relationships, and build meaningful connections within the school community. The event was held on June 27, 2026, and was open to the entire school community.

Project KALINISAN

Project KALINISAN is a school cleanliness initiative that promotes discipline, cleanliness, and environmental responsibility among students through daily classroom inspections. Classes are assessed based on their cleanliness and orderliness, with recognition and rewards given at the end of each term to encourage active participation and consistency in maintaining a clean learning environment. The activity involves the entire school community and is conducted through daily inspections and regular awarding ceremonies.

Teachers' Day Celebration

The Teachers' Day Celebration is an annual event dedicated to recognizing and appreciating the dedication, commitment, and invaluable contributions of teachers to the school community. It provides students with an opportunity to express their gratitude through various activities and gestures of appreciation. The celebration was held on October 5, 2026, and involved the entire school community.

Award for Student Organization Achievement

The Supreme Secondary Learner Government (SSLG) received the Award for Student Organization Achievement during the Corcuera National High School Awards for School Years 2024–2025 and 2025–2026. This recognition highlights the organization's outstanding contributions, active involvement, and accomplishments in implementing student-centered programs and initiatives that support student development and promote a positive school community.
