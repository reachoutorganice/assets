Be a Member of Filipino Club!
Who can join the organization?

All students of Corcuera National High School who are interested in promoting Filipino language, culture, traditions, and literature. 

Membership Requirements

- Currently enrolled student of Corcuera National High School.
- Interested in Filipino language, culture, and traditions.
- Willing to participate in club activities and events.
- Responsible and respectful toward fellow members. 

How can students join? 

Students may join by contacting the Filipino Club officers or adviser, completing the membership registration form, and participating in the club’s activities. 

Recruitment / Application Period

 During the opening of the school year and announced recruitment periods.
