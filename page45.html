Mission

Our mission is to educate and empower young people to become responsible, compassionate, and active volunteers who promote humanity, help people in need, and provide service to the community. 

Vision

A community of young, empowered, and dedicated volunteers united by the spirit of humanity, kindness, and service, working toward a safer and healthier society.

Main Objectives / Goals

1. Promote Humanity – Encourage kindness, compassion, and respect for others.
2. Develop Volunteerism – Inspire students to participate in community service and humanitarian activities.
3. Provide First Aid Awareness – Teach students basic first aid and emergency response skills.
4. Support Disaster Preparedness – Educate members on disaster risk reduction and preparedness.
5. Promote Health and Safety – Encourage healthy practices and awareness within the school and community.
6. Develop Leadership – Build students' confidence, teamwork, discipline, and leadership skills.
