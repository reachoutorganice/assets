Intramural Meet 2026

The Red Cross Youth members will provide health and safety assistance to ensure the well-being of students, teachers, athletes, and other participants throughout the Intramural Meet. They will help monitor the event, provide basic first aid to individuals experiencing minor injuries or discomfort, and assist in responding to medical emergencies. The activity also promotes safety awareness and highlights the importance of preparedness, teamwork, and volunteerism. It will be conducted once every school year, with students, teachers, and athletes as the target participants.
